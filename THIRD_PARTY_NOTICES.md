## Four-case promotional reel

`showcase-*`, `chroma-*`, `ridgeline-*`, `pawpaw-*` and `seismic-*` are edited screen captures. They contain no downloadable demo source, original asset library, user profile or account credentials. The original skill's MIT license does not relicense third-party content depicted in these captures.

- **CHROMA:** React Bits ColorBends and BlurText, Animate UI Button, Lucide icons, Three.js and Motion. The background is the running ColorBends shader.
- **RIDGELINE:** React Bits Threads, ScrollReveal, BlurText and TiltedCard; Animate UI; Radix Themes; Lucide. Mountain and outdoor photos come from Unsplash under its [license](https://unsplash.com/license). Products and brand descriptions are fictional concept content.
- **PAWPAW:** Radix Themes, Animate UI, Motion and Phosphor icons; Unsplash pet photos; OpenMoji product illustrations. All depicted account, pet and order details are fictional demonstration data.
- **Research presentation:** Vanta Waves, Three.js, GSAP SplitText, UIkit, Apache ECharts and KaTeX. The source is Wang, Chen & Cheng (2025), [Physics-Guided Self-Supervised Seismic Impedance Inversion](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11004-025-10215-3). The selected footage uses the interface's teaching graphs and plotted numeric results. The paper's PDF and original figure files are not included. The publisher [retains article rights](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11004-025-10215-3#rightslink).
- **Video composition:** HyperFrames, GSAP, DM Sans and Noto Sans SC. Font files and third-party runtime sources are not bundled with the published reel.
